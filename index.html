Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know about the BLONDY rewards program.

How long does it take to receive my VP?
Once you complete the review and pass Cloudflare verification, VP is credited instantly to your linked Riot account. In 98% of cases it takes less than 3 minutes.
Is this a real Riot Games program?
BLONDY is an independent community initiative run by verified partners. We buy VP in bulk and distribute it to users who help us improve our tools through honest feedback.
Why do you need Cloudflare verification?
To prevent abuse and bot accounts from claiming rewards. Every user goes through the same process — it confirms you're a real human before credit is issued.
Do I need to give my account password?
No. You only provide your Riot ID (Username#TAG). We never ask for passwords, 2FA codes, or any sensitive data.
Can I do this more than once?
Currently the program is limited to one reward per Riot account. Multiple reviews from the same account will be rejected during verification.
What if my VP doesn't arrive?
Contact our support team via the Discord link in the footer. 99% of delayed payouts are resolved within 24 hours.
